Zero downloads, zero complicated setup. Works right inside any standard modern web browser.

Open Camera or Speak

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about ARTH's privacy, models, and usage.

Is my camera video recorded or sent to a cloud server? ▼

No. ARTH is engineered with 100% on-device client-side execution. Camera video streams never leave your browser or get stored on any remote server, ensuring complete confidentiality.

Which sign language dialects does ARTH support? ▼

ARTH currently supports American Sign Language (ASL) and Indian Sign Language (ISL), along with universal signs and full A-Z fingerspelling. You can toggle between dialects with one click.

Can I use ARTH without an active webcam? ▼

Yes! ARTH features an Interactive Vision Simulation mode with one-click test gestures, full Voice-to-Sign translation, Emergency SOS flashcards, and a searchable Dictionary.

Is ARTH free to use? ▼

Yes, ARTH is completely free, open, and dedicated to the global Deaf & Hard-of-Hearing community.
